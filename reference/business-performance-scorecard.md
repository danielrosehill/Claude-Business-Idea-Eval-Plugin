# Scoring Framework: PRGX (Performance, Resilience, Growth, conteXtual Fit)

**Purpose:**
PRGX evaluates **existing businesses** — ventures the user is already running or invested in. While ICEC asks "should I pursue this idea?", PRGX asks "how is this business actually doing and where should I focus?"

---

## 1. Scoring Dimensions (1-10 scale)

### Performance (P)

Score how well the business is currently executing against its objectives.

Consider:
* Revenue trajectory (growing, flat, declining)
* Profitability or path to profitability
* Customer acquisition and retention metrics
* Operational efficiency
* Product-market fit signals (churn, NPS, repeat usage)
* Delivery quality and consistency

---

### Resilience (R)

Score how well-positioned the business is to withstand shocks and market changes.

Consider:
* Revenue concentration (single customer dependency)
* Market diversification
* Cash reserves and runway
* Competitive moat strength
* Regulatory or legal exposure
* Team/key-person dependency
* Supply chain or technology dependency risks

---

### Growth (G)

Score the realistic growth potential from the current position.

Consider:
* Total addressable market remaining
* Scalability of current model
* Expansion opportunities (new markets, products, channels)
* Organic growth momentum
* Ability to attract capital if needed
* Network effects or compounding advantages

---

### Contextual Fit (X)

Score how well the business continues to align with the owner's life circumstances — identical philosophy to ICEC's Contextual Suitability.

Consider:
* **Time demands vs. availability**: Is the business consuming more time than the owner can sustain?
* **Financial alignment**: Is the business providing adequate returns relative to opportunity cost?
* **Energy and motivation**: Does the owner still have drive for this venture?
* **Life stage compatibility**: Has the owner's life changed since starting?
* **Skills utilization**: Is the owner's expertise being well-deployed?
* **Strategic alignment**: Does this still fit the owner's broader goals and portfolio?

---

## 2. Final Score Calculation

### Equal-Weight Formula (default):

```
PRGX = (P + R + G + X) / 4
```

---

## 3. Interpretation of PRGX Scores

* **8.0-10.0 -> Double down**
  High-performing, resilient business with strong growth and good owner fit. Invest more.

* **6.5-7.9 -> Optimize**
  Solid business with specific areas to improve. Identify and fix weak dimensions.

* **5.0-6.4 -> Restructure or pivot**
  Significant issues in one or more dimensions. Needs strategic intervention.

* **Below 5.0 -> Exit or wind down**
  Poor performance across dimensions. Consider selling, pivoting dramatically, or closing.

---

## 4. Recommended Actions by Score Profile

Beyond the aggregate score, the **pattern** of individual scores matters:

| Pattern | Interpretation | Action |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| High P, Low G | Cash cow, limited upside | Harvest profits, diversify |
| Low P, High G | Underperforming with potential | Fix operations, then scale |
| High everything, Low X | Great business, wrong owner | Consider selling or hiring operator |
| Low R, everything else OK | Fragile success | Shore up resilience before growing |
| Low P, Low R | Distressed | Urgent intervention or exit |

---

## 5. Extended Analysis Dimensions

In addition to PRGX scores, evaluate:

### Financial Health
- Revenue, margins, burn rate, runway
- Unit economics and customer lifetime value
- Working capital and cash flow patterns

### Operational Assessment
- Team capacity and capability
- Process maturity
- Technology infrastructure
- Customer support quality

### Market Position
- Competitive dynamics and market share
- Brand strength and reputation
- Customer concentration and diversification
- Pricing power

### Strategic Options
- Acquisition potential (acquirer or target)
- Partnership opportunities
- Pivot possibilities
- Exit scenarios and valuations
